About this app

ARCHIVEFLIX is a free, open home screen for the public catalog at archive.org. It plays titles through the Archive's own player and links downloads from the Archive's own files. Progress is marked by you, because the Archive player cannot report where you are in a film.

The Archive player cannot report where you are, so mark your own place here. It powers Continue Watching and Resume.

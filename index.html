IMDb ratings (optional)

The Top IMDb row uses real IMDb ratings, and cards can show real movie posters, only when you add a free OMDb key. The key stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere except omdbapi.com. Without a key, that row falls back to Archive ratings and says so.

Get a free key at omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx, then paste it here.

TMDB posters (optional)

Add a free TMDB API key and cards can show real movie posters from TMDB. The key stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere except api.themoviedb.org. Without a key, posters come from the Archive and Wikimedia Commons instead.

Get a free key at themoviedb.org/settings/api, then paste it here.

Install this app

Put ARCHIVEFLIX on your home screen and it opens like a real app, full screen, with its own icon. No app store and no terminal needed.

On an Android phone: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dot menu, then Add to Home screen. On an iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Install button above appears when your browser offers it.

Your data

History, My List, likes and settings live in this browser only. Export a backup to move them to another browser or device. A backup includes all profiles.

About this app

ARCHIVEFLIX is a free, open home screen for the public catalog at archive.org. It plays the Archive's own video files right in the app and saves your place automatically as you watch, so you pick up where you left off. Downloads link to the Archive's own files. A few titles have no file this browser can play directly; those play through the Archive's own player instead.

How to use

  1. Profiles. Tap your name in the top bar to switch profiles, or add one under Profiles above. Each profile keeps its own history, My List, likes and theme.
  2. PINs. Set a PIN on a profile under Profiles to lock it. You are asked for the PIN each time you open that profile. A PIN is a casual lock for this device, not encryption.
  3. My List. Open any title and tap Add to My List to save it for later. Saved titles show under My List in the top bar.
  4. Reactions. Tap Like on a title you enjoy and the home page suggests more like it. Tap Not for me and that title stops appearing in rows and Browse.
  5. OMDb and TMDB keys. Paste a free OMDb key above to see real IMDb ratings and posters, and a free TMDB key for more posters. Keys stay in this browser only.
  6. Install. On Android, open this page in Chrome, tap the three dot menu, then Add to Home screen. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
